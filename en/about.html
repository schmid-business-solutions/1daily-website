About us

Precise in every trade. Scalable by design.

We trade short, precise moves in the gold market, using our own software and clear rules. Every trading day delivers data that improves our system. More capital, more markets and our software as a product make the model scalable.

How we work

Analysis. Execution. Protection. In that order.

01
AI-powered analysis

Goldcheck

Six layers (technical, institutional, macro, news, confidence and signal) produce a clear assessment per timeframe, from Strong Sell to Strong Buy, with the key reasons.

02
Human execution

Trader

Experienced traders decide based on the signals and execute short, targeted trades with a predefined entry, stop-loss and target.

03
Systematic capital protection

1daily Guard

1daily Guard sits between trader and broker and enforces the risk rules automatically: defined risk per trade, a limited number of positions, a stop-loss on every order.

Founder & Board Member
