Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. This privacy policy explains which personal data 1daily AG processes when you visit this website. It is based on the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, takes account of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Controller

1daily AG, Hardeggweg 2, 3612 Steffisburg, Switzerland. For data protection matters, contact us via the contact form or by post at the address above.

2. Visiting the website (hosting)

This website is served via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, technically necessary data such as IP address, date and time, page requested and browser type are processed in the host’s server log files in order to deliver the website securely and reliably. We do not analyse this data ourselves.

3. Contact and investor forms

When you submit a form, we process the data you provide (e.g. name, e-mail, phone, country of residence, investor type, message) solely to handle your request or expression of interest and to contact you. Submissions are transmitted via Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA), which forwards the form data to us by e-mail. The data is deleted once it is no longer needed for this purpose and no statutory retention obligations apply.

4. Transfers abroad

GitHub and Formspree are based in the USA. Transfers take place on the basis of appropriate safeguards, in particular the Swiss-U.S. or EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (where the provider is certified) or standard contractual clauses.

5. Cookies, fonts and local storage

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies or any analytics services. Fonts are loaded directly from our own server; no connection to Google Fonts is made. Your language choice (German/English) is stored only locally in your browser (localStorage) and is not transmitted to us.

6. Your rights

Within the scope of applicable law, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Please contact us at the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or another competent supervisory authority.

7. Changes

We may amend this privacy policy at any time. The version published on this website applies.
